Your privacy

Speech to text runs on this device. Your recordings are not uploaded or saved.

The first time, Vokal downloads the speech model from huggingface.co and the speech runtime from cdn.jsdelivr.net.

Voices marked "sends text online to speak" send the words to that voice's provider. Other voices work on this device.

Set up Vokal

Add Vokal to your Home Screen

On iPhone, add Vokal to your Home Screen first and set it up there. iOS may keep the home-screen app's data apart from Safari's, so a model downloaded here might have to download again.

  1. Tap Share.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
